Meetings & Events

Field walks, outings, and observations.

Updates from Upstate SC Young Birders meetings, bird walks, and informal outings around the Upstate.

Upcoming Meeting

June 25, 2026

Conestee Nature Preserve Bird Walk

Our June meeting will be held at Conestee Nature Preserve at 8:30 a.m. Members of the Greenville County Bird Club will be guiding us around the preserve and helping us identify birds.

We hope to see wetland birds such as Red-winged Blackbirds, herons, and Swamp Sparrows, as well as woodland birds like Red-eyed Vireos and a variety of warblers.

Informal Walk

June 9, 2026

Table Rock State Park Bird Walk

Since there was a long gap between official meetings, we planned an informal bird walk at Table Rock State Park along the Lakeside Trail.

The outing included introductions, a short walk, time to chat, and a few bird identification games.

Past Meeting

May 2026

Clemson Botanical Garden Bird Walk

Morning walk through the South Carolina Botanical Garden with the Winged Wednesday group. Participants practiced bird identification, photography, and observation skills.

Species observed included:

  • Carolina Wren
  • Northern Mockingbird
  • Eastern Bluebird
  • Red-bellied Woodpecker
  • Canada Goose
