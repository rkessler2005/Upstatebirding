June 25, 2026

Conestee Nature Preserve Bird Walk

Our June meeting will be held at Conestee Nature Preserve at 8:30 a.m. Members of the Greenville County Bird Club (GCBC) will be guiding us around the preserve and helping us identify birds.

Betsy Lewis, one of our mentors and the president of GCBC, helped organize this outing. We are grateful to Betsy and the other GCBC members for sharing their time and knowledge with our group.

We hope to see wetland birds such as Red-winged Blackbirds, herons, and Swamp Sparrows, as well as woodland birds like Red-eyed Vireos and a variety of warblers.

June 9, 2026

Table Rock State Park Bird Walk

Since there was a long gap between official meetings, we organized an informal bird walk at Table Rock State Park along the Lakeside Trail.

Participants introduced themselves, enjoyed a short walk around the lake, discussed bird identification, and spent time birding together in a relaxed setting.

The outing provided an opportunity for members to connect before the next official club meeting while exploring one of South Carolina's most scenic state parks.

May 2026

Clemson Botanical Garden Bird Walk

Morning walk through the South Carolina Botanical Garden with the Winged Wednesday group. Participants practiced bird identification, photography, and observation skills.

Species observed included:

  • Carolina Wren
  • Northern Mockingbird
  • Eastern Bluebird
  • Red-bellied Woodpecker
  • Canada Goose
